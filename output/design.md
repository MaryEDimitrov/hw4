# Campus Customs design

## What changed

- Added a Yale-inspired blue, navy, cream, white, and gold palette with YaleNew/Mallory-first typography.
- Created an asymmetric editorial product grid with varied image sizes and stronger visual hierarchy.
- Improved product images with full-garment, contained presentation instead of awkward cropping.
- Added consistent detail pages, clickable chat product cards, a cart drawer, account status, and logout.
- Added concise loading, error, empty, focus, responsive, and reduced-motion states.

## Why it helps shoppers stay

The storefront feels credible and memorable, products are easier to compare, and full details are always one click away. Chat makes discovery easier, the cart lets shoppers save ideas, and account memory reduces repeated effort for returning customers. Clear feedback and responsive layouts keep the experience comfortable instead of confusing.

## Why it helps the business

The design encourages browsing, product discovery, and return visits while creating a foundation for checkout, favorites, recommendations, and customer analytics.
